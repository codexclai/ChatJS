# Community Code of Conduct

ChatJS aims to be a respectful, constructive, and accessible engineering space.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and implementation details, not people.
- Explain technical disagreements with evidence and useful alternatives.
- Welcome good-faith questions and different experience levels.
- Keep discussions focused, professional, and free of harassment.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, intellectual property, and third-party terms.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, malicious disclosure of private information, and repeated bad-faith conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report serious conduct issues privately to the repository owner through GitHub's repository contact or reporting mechanisms. Do not publish personal information or security-sensitive details in an issue.

The maintainer may edit, remove, or close content and restrict participation when needed to protect contributors and the project. Enforcement should be proportionate, documented, and consistent.

This code of conduct is an operational community policy, not a substitute for GitHub's Terms of Service or applicable law.
